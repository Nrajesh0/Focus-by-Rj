<p><b>Focus by Rj</b> is a strictly offline, privacy-first Android application designed to help you stay focused and manage screen time without compromising your personal data.</p>

<p><b>Features:</b></p>
<ul>
    <li>Block distracting apps using powerful App Restrictions.</li>
    <li>Schedule Deep Work sessions with timers.</li>
    <li>Schedule automatic locking routines (e.g., Bedtime or Work hours).</li>
    <li> usage statistics.</li>
    <li>Secure fallback mechanisms, and device administrator protections to prevent circumvention.</li>
</ul>

<p>This application is fully open source (GPLv3), contains zero analytics or tracking software, and operates 100% offline. All data is stored locally on your device.</p>
